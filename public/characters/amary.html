Amary Autem Libera


Quiet, pensive, and focused — Amary likens herself to that of a tenacious pragmatic. Descending from a once aristocratic lineage of Vulra, her convictions have driven her to exile, seeking answers over every enticement that privilege has placed in her path.

Although her outwards demeanour presents a stoic and somewhat cold appearance, she is possessed of a certain anxiety, and much prefers to maintain a small and intimate circle of acquaintance. Do not mistake her awkwardness for lack of skill, for out on the field, hers do not falter.
